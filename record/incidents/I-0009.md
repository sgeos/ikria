# I-0009, Bridge Deadline During Parachute Descent

> **Navigation**: [Incidents](README.md) | [Verification evidence](../verification/VR-0012.md)

**Date and author.** 2026-11-22, Codex.
**Classification.** Runtime timeout, underlying stall unresolved.
**Status.** Open. Empty article landed passively and was recovered with all eight parts.

## Observation and consequence

The normal TP-0003 hop used Ikria `7907697` and bridge `ef85dd8`. It issued one
ignition and one parachute deployment. After 3,558 acknowledged commands, the bridge
exited with `Elapsed(())`. The last sample, sequence 3557, was descending at 410.842 m
with eight parts, no crew and a deployed parachute. The harness recorded a failed
session and no completion event. The refused-boost test was deferred.

The connection ended about 149 wall seconds after log creation. The handshake had
completed, and the bridge's outer session limit is 900 seconds. Source inspection
therefore identifies the 700 ms cycle deadline as the timeout source. Its exact
operation and underlying stall are unknown. The harness codec error is consistent
with the peer disconnecting and does not establish an oversized message.

No ignition or deployment acknowledgement was missing. No command was retried.
Independent observation later confirmed a settled landing over 4.46 game seconds.
Recovery returned to the verification Space Center. All four kerbals were Available,
and the career file hash remained unchanged.

## Cause and limits of evidence

The bridge applies one deadline across tick acquisition, telemetry, socket exchange,
hold validation, command application and acknowledgement. Its error carries no cycle
or operation context. The journal cannot identify which unsampled operation stalled.
The next game-log autosave occurred after the disconnection. Host scheduling, game
pauses and individual remote calls are hypotheses, not established causes.

The successful passive landing does not qualify transport loss as a safe abort. The
parachute had already deployed. A disconnect earlier in the trajectory can leave the
vehicle without a recovery action.

## Containment and acceptance

The failed journal, process diagnostics and independent landing observations are
retained in VR-0012. Replay refuses to certify the failed journal. No deadline or
arming rule was relaxed, and no further flight followed.

Add cycle and operation diagnostics without changing the timeout boundary. Preserve
the rule that a cancelled kRPC connection is discarded without cleanup calls on that
connection. Diagnose the stall and demonstrate a complete normal flight before
resuming the refused-boost case. Both flights require acknowledged actions, settled
touchdown and successful journals. Repeatability remains a separate qualification.
