# I-0003, Abort Pod Destroyed at Ignition by Overlapping Motors

> **Navigation**: [Incidents](README.md) | [Verification evidence](../verification/VR-0011.md)

**Date and author.** 2026-11-18, a Claude line.
**Classification.** Design fault under R10, not a game defect.
**Status.** Corrected in revision A2C and checked before every later flight.

## Timeline and consequence

The first pad abort of IKR-SEP-A2B decoupled its uncrewed Mk1 pod from the unlit Flea
and fired four sepratrons. The pod was destroyed within seconds, near the pad, and its
vessel record remained with no parts. The script stopped on the destroyed pod and saved
no log. No kerbal was aboard, which the roster confirmed.

## Cause and contributing factors

The sepratrons were mounted 0.15 m below the pod's origin. Their 0.538 m length reached
0.05 m into the TR-18A decoupler, which stays with the booster. At ignition the motors
were therefore inside another vessel's collider *(strong inference from the bounding-box
check, which is conservative)*. The craft generator computed positions without checking
neighbours.

## Corrective action and acceptance

`tools/sepratron/diag_overlap.py` reads each part's bounding box through kRPC and reports
any sepratron overlapping a part other than its parent, with nothing fired. A2C raised
the motors to the pod's origin and showed no overlap, and its pad aborts flew cleanly.
The design rule is in the program document under separation.
