# I-0006, Lander Destroyed by a Velocity Set in a Moving Frame

> **Navigation**: [Incidents](README.md) | [Verification evidence](../verification/VR-0011.md)

**Date and author.** 2026-11-18, a Claude line.
**Classification.** Test-software fault under R10, of the same class as I-0001.
**Status.** Corrected, and a plausibility check refuses to continue on a wrong placement.

## Timeline and consequence

A descent-braking run placed IKR-SEP-A3C 4 km above the Mun and set its velocity to fall
at 200 m/s. The article was destroyed at once, reading tens of kilometres per second.

## Cause and contributing factors

The velocity was passed in the vessel's own surface frame, which moves and turns with
the vessel, so the requested 200 m/s became a nonsensical velocity. I-0001 recorded the
same class of error, a velocity taken in a moving frame, and it recurred here. Two later
attempts also left empty vessel records after a debug placement with a 4 km height.

## Corrective action and acceptance

Placement now uses `set_flight`, which sets altitude, speed and attitude together, and
the script refuses to fire unless the vessel has its parts and a vertical speed within
15 m/s of the request. The descent-braking run then shed 132.60 m/s against 132.94 m/s
predicted. Any velocity a script sets or reads is stated with its frame.
