# I-0004, Abort Pod Lost with a Parachute Set to Open at 50 m

> **Navigation**: [Incidents](README.md) | [Verification evidence](../verification/VR-0011.md)

**Date and author.** 2026-11-18, a Claude line.
**Classification.** Configuration fault under R10, not a game defect.
**Status.** Corrected in revision A2D by resetting parts to their defaults.

## Timeline and consequence

The first pad abort of IKR-SEP-A2C escaped cleanly and the parachute was armed, but
the pod struck the ground about 480 m from the pad and was destroyed. A Flight Results
dialog reported a catastrophic failure. The script had run past its time limit and its
output was buffered, so no log survived. A repeat with a safety net showed the
parachute only semi-deployed at 300 m, falling at 103 m/s.

## Cause and contributing factors

The parachute's block was copied from the stock Skywinder AE1, whose designer set it to
open at 50 m with a minimum air pressure of 0.75. The part's defaults are 1,000 m and
0.04. The generator then reset only thrust and resources. Contributing were a time limit
shorter than the script's longest path and output lost to buffering.

## Corrective action and acceptance

The generator now resets every module field the part configuration defines to its
value. A2D's parachute opened fully and its pod touched down at 5.0 m/s. Scripts now
flush their output, save after each phase, and run within limits that exceed their
longest path. A safety net sets a pod down through the debug service if it is still
fast near the ground.
