# I-0002, Touchdown Bounce Stopped the Harness

> **Navigation**: [Incidents](README.md) | [Verification evidence](../verification/VR-0010.md)

**Date and author.** 2026-11-17, Codex.
**Classification.** Control-software fault under R10.
**Status.** Corrected and regression-tested.

## Timeline and consequence

M-0001 hop 2 landed with all eight parts. At sequence 6,735 the game classified the
article as landed with upward velocity of about 2.26 metres per second. One tick
later it classified the bouncing article as flying. The program had latched its
completion phase, while the host correctly refused completion in flight. The session
ended without claiming success. The article settled and was recovered intact.

## Cause and correction

The program treated the first landed classification as final. It now returns from
the completion candidate to descent when the situation changes. The host resets its
settlement timer on bounce or vertical speed of at least 0.5 metres per second and
requires a continuous second of settled ground contact.

Unit tests exercise the phase transition and a complete session containing a bounce.
The later normal and coast-abort flights completed the settled-contact interval and
were recovered intact. No career criterion or science award is credited. The failed
journal remains available and cannot pass the replay certification path.
