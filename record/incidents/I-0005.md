# I-0005, Abort Pod Lost While a Booster Watch Ran Alone

> **Navigation**: [Incidents](README.md) | [Verification evidence](../verification/VR-0011.md)

**Date and author.** 2026-11-18, a Claude line.
**Classification.** Test-software fault under R10.
**Status.** Corrected in the flight-abort script.

## Timeline and consequence

The first in-flight abort of IKR-SEP-A2D escaped the still-burning Flea, and the gap
grew to 5.4 km. The pod struck the ground with its parachute unarmed and was
destroyed. The booster's return toward the pod, to about 354 m, was recorded.

## Cause and contributing factors

The script watched the booster in its own loop for a fixed 45 s and only then looked
for the apex. The pod's apex came at about 20 s and its fall ended at about 45 s, so
nothing armed the parachute in time.

## Corrective action and acceptance

One loop now does every task on every tick, the booster watch, the apex and the
parachute, and the safety net. The repeat flight abort escaped, landed at 5.2 m/s and was
recovered. The design rule is in the program document under abort.
