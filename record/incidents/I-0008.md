# I-0008, Verification Placement Did Not Reach an Admissible Pad State

> **Navigation**: [Incidents](README.md) | [Verification evidence](../verification/VR-0012.md)

**Date and author.** 2026-11-22, Codex.
**Classification.** Preparation anomaly, cause unresolved. No game-defect ruling.
**Status.** Open for placement reliability. Manual workaround demonstrated once.
No crew or article loss from this anomaly.

## Timeline and consequence

Crewed placement of IKR-TS-001B returned PreLaunch. After Jebediah left and was
recovered, the empty article reported Landed. Its fuel, inactive motor and stowed
parachute remained unchanged. Since arming requires PreLaunch, only a telemetry
session was run. It passed and the eight-part article was recovered.

A direct empty-crew placement then timed out after 60 seconds. The operator identified
Warning No Control with Launch anyway and Cancel buttons. After the operator reported
the Space Center, a new snapshot showed no test article. A new request allowed 180
seconds for interaction and also timed out. A final snapshot again showed no test
article. All four kerbals were Available throughout the post-recovery observations.

Armed verification was initially blocked. The operator subsequently placed an empty
article through the editor. Guarded inspection confirmed the required PreLaunch state,
and two pad checks passed before a normal hop encountered the separate I-0009 timeout.
The unsuccessful attempts and continuation remain in VR-0012. The career file was unchanged.

## Facts and hypotheses

The Landed classification after crew removal and the warning during direct uncrewed
placement are observed facts. The warning is consistent with the empty capsule having
no control source. The exact event that changed PreLaunch to Landed is not established.
It is also not established whether accepting the warning before the request deadline
would complete placement. Neither timeout proves that the game itself hung.

## Containment and acceptance

The existing arming and ignition rules remain unchanged. No ignition was requested,
and each uncertain placement was followed by an independent snapshot inspection.
The second request was made only after inspection showed no test article from the first.
The operator was asked to cancel any remaining warning after the second timeout.

A guarded uncrewed-placement option now logs intent, waits at most 180 seconds and
checks the returned state. Offline tests reject an unexpected craft, crew, Landed
state or active engine. This is instrumentation and a guard, not a verified remedy.

Close this incident only after a documented procedure reliably produces the empty
PreLaunch article with the operator interaction recorded, and the planned armed
checks can begin without relaxing the profile. Retain negative evidence and distinguish
any warning response from actual placement success.

Manual editor placement met the profile once on 2026-11-22 without any policy change.
The checkpoint `ipv-flight-fix-manual-pad-20261122` preserves that state. Repeated
placement and successful use of the guarded automated alternative remain unverified.
