# VR-0006, Keleusma step time

> **Navigation**: [Verification](./README.md)

**Phase 0 experiments.** 8.

**Purpose.** Measure what one step of a representative Keleusma control loop costs on
this machine, and the verifier's bound for it.

**Configuration.** Keleusma 0.2.2 from crates.io, the `ikria-harness` crate at the commit
that added it, built in release with Rust 1.100.0 on an arm64 Mac under macOS 27.2.1.

**Procedure.** `cargo run --release -p ikria-harness --example keleusma_timing` built the
attitude loop of `crates/ikria-harness/src/attitude.rs`, ran 10,000 warm-up steps and
then 200,000 timed steps, and compared each command with the Rust reference.

**Results.**
- No mismatch against the reference in 200,000 steps.
- A step took a median of 2.29 µs, a mean of 2.52 µs, 2.71 µs at the 99th percentile,
  41.5 µs at the 99.9th, and 1.84 ms at most.
- The verifier bounds one iteration at 67 nominal cycles.

**Deviations.** None.

**Conclusions.** About 8,700 such steps fit a 20 ms tick at the median and 481 at the
99.9th percentile, so the harness's limit is the bridge, at about 0.26 ms per kRPC call,
not Keleusma. The worst case is most likely the operating system scheduling the
process *(inference)*.
