# VR-0005, The science subject inventory

> **Navigation**: [Verification](./README.md)

**Phase 0 experiments.** 7.

**Purpose.** Count the science subjects the game offers, and their value on Hard, as the
baseline of the science ledger.

**Configuration.** Kerbal Space Program 1.12.5 with both expansions, kRPC 0.7.0 server
and Python client, on an arm64 Mac under macOS 27.2.1. Save "Ikria Simulated Research and
Dev", a sandbox on Hard. Probe scripts are in [`tools/phase0/`](../../tools/phase0/README.md),
in their final versions, and raw outputs in [`data/`](data/). Runs took place on
2026-11-14, in Coordinated Universal Time.

**Procedure.**
1. `probe_inventory_tick.py` read every body's radius, gravity, atmosphere and biomes
   from the running game into `data/inventory.json`.
2. `science_subjects.py` combined those with the experiments' caps and masks from the
   game's `ScienceDefs.cfg`, the official wiki's body multipliers, and the wiki's 37
   Space Center location biomes.

**Results.**
- 17 bodies, with 145 biomes between them, not counting the location biomes.
- About 3,104 subjects, worth about 217,818 science on Hard. Without Breaking Ground's
  34 surface features, that is about 361,529 on Normal, 92 percent of the wiki's
  392,019.

**Assumptions.**
- Splashed subjects exist only on Kerbin, Eve and Laythe.
- The location biomes count for landed experiments only, at Kerbin's landed multiplier.
- Surface features count at their own caps times 0.6, with no body multiplier.
- Asteroid and comet samples, deployed experiments and the laboratory are left out.

**Deviations.** A first version of the surface-feature total used a wrong split of the
34 features by cap, which happened to give the same sum. It was corrected.

**Conclusions.** The tree's 18,468 science is about 8.5 percent of what the game offers
on Hard. The difference from the wiki's total lies in the assumptions above, and the
science ledger will replace the count with the save's own subjects.
