## Findings about the tools

- `Debug.unlock_technology_tree` researched every node in kRPC 0.7.0 but left no part
  purchased in the save, contrary to its documentation.
- `launch_vessel` takes the crew list fourth, so arguments are passed by keyword.
- The autopilot is engaged with `engaged = True`. It has no `engage` method.
- `set_landed` with a height of 4 km left an empty vessel record twice, while
  `set_flight` placed a falling vessel correctly, including on the airless Mun.
- `set_velocity` takes the frame it is given literally. Passing the vessel's own surface
  frame, which moves and turns with it, destroyed an article, I-0006.
- A Flight Results dialog after the loss of the active vessel blocks `launch_vessel`
  until the operator closes it.
- `launch_vessel(recover=True)` with a crewed pod on the pad left its kerbal Missing,
  I-0007 *(strong inference)*.

## Not verified

Repeatability beyond the pad abort, crewed flight, the ejectable cockpit, a full-thrust
soft landing, the editor slider's step size, and exhaust heating of neighbouring parts.
