# VR-0004, Physics range of a vessel that is not active

> **Navigation**: [Verification](./README.md)

**Phase 0 experiments.** 9, in part.

**Purpose.** Establish whether a vessel flying in the atmosphere, away from the active
vessel, stays simulated or is put on rails or removed, which is the booster program's
main risk.

**Configuration.** Kerbal Space Program 1.12.5 with both expansions, kRPC 0.7.0 server
and Python client, on an arm64 Mac under macOS 27.2.1. Save "Ikria Simulated Research and
Dev", a sandbox on Hard. Probe scripts are in [`tools/phase0/`](../../tools/phase0/README.md),
in their final versions, and raw outputs in [`data/`](data/). Runs took place on
2026-11-14, in Coordinated Universal Time.

**Procedure.**
1. `probe_range.py` sent the kerbal of `IkriaT0` outside, so that the kerbal on the
   ground was the active vessel. It lit the empty pod's Flea through `Engine.active`,
   and logged the pod's distance, altitude, speed, `loaded`, `packed` and situation four
   times a second.
2. `probe_range2.py` tried the same with the empty `IkriaT1`, lighting its first-stage
   engines through their parts.

**Results.**
- The pod climbed to 12.8 km altitude and 12.7 km from the kerbal. It stayed loaded and
  off rails throughout, and was destroyed on impact about 115 s after ignition rather
  than removed in flight. The default `physics_range` kRPC reported before launch was
  350 m, which therefore does not govern flight in the atmosphere.
- `activate_next_stage` refused a vessel that was not active. Lighting the engine
  through its part worked.
- `SpaceCenter.vessels` left out kerbals outside a vessel, flags and asteroids, which a
  save file listed, but did list debris.
- `IkriaT1` has no probe core, so with its kerbal outside nothing held its attitude. It
  tipped over at 230 m and crashed about 550 m from the kerbal.

**Deviations.**
- The first run of `probe_range.py` used staging and failed on the refusal above. Later
  runs lit the engine through its part.
- The `IkriaT1` trial did not reach the distances it was meant to test.

**Conclusions.** In Kerbin's atmosphere a vessel that is not active stays simulated out
to at least 12.7 km by default. Beyond that, and with a raised physics range, the test
moves to the booster's development step V3, the first vehicle able to hold its own
attitude far from the active one. A separated booster is commanded through its parts,
not through staging.
