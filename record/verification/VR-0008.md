# VR-0008, Orbital hazard bands of the moons

> **Navigation**: [Verification](./README.md)

**Phase 0 experiments.** None. This report supports `docs/architecture/HAZARDS.md`.

**Purpose.** Compute, for every moon, the band of distances from its parent where an
orbit can enter the moon's sphere of influence, after the operator noted on 2026-11-14
that Ike can throw vessels around in orbit.

**Configuration.** Kerbal Space Program 1.12.5, kRPC 0.7.0 server and Python client, on
an arm64 Mac under macOS 27.2.1, read-only against the loaded save on 2026-11-14 in
Coordinated Universal Time. The script is
[`tools/phase0/orbit_hazards.py`](../../tools/phase0/README.md), and its output is
[`data/orbit_hazards.json`](data/orbit_hazards.json).

**Procedure.** The script read each body's orbit, its semi-major axis, eccentricity and
inclination, and its sphere of influence, then computed the band
`[a(1 - e) - SOI, a(1 + e) + SOI]` as altitude above the parent's surface.

**Results.** The bands are in `HAZARDS.md` and the data file. Ike's runs from 1,734 to
4,026 km above Duna, and Tylo's from 51,643 to 73,357 km above Jool.

**Deviations.** None.

**Conclusions.** Ike and Tylo are the pitfall bodies of the operator's kind. The band is
conservative, since an inclined orbit can pass above or below a moon inside its band.
