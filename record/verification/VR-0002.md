# VR-0002, Lockstep throughput for one and two vessels

> **Navigation**: [Verification](./README.md)

**Phase 0 experiments.** 3 and 10.

**Purpose.** Measure the wall time of one held tick in which the client reads a vessel's
telemetry and writes its controls, for one vessel and for two.

**Configuration.** Kerbal Space Program 1.12.5 with both expansions, kRPC 0.7.0 server
and Python client, on an arm64 Mac under macOS 27.2.1. Save "Ikria Simulated Research and
Dev", a sandbox on Hard. Probe scripts are in [`tools/phase0/`](../../tools/phase0/README.md),
in their final versions, and raw outputs in [`data/`](data/). Runs took place on
2026-11-14, in Coordinated Universal Time.

**Procedure.**
1. One vessel. `probe_pad_eva.py` launched the test craft `IkriaT0`, a copy of the stock
   Jumping Flea, to the pad. In 100 held cycles it made 15 calls each, reading position,
   velocity, rotation, mass, thrust and flight data, and writing throttle, pitch, yaw and
   roll.
2. Two vessels. `probe_range2.py` launched `IkriaT1`, a copy of the stock Kerbal 1, sent
   its kerbal outside, and in 50 held cycles read and commanded both the kerbal and the
   rocket, 16 calls each.

**Results.**

| Vessels | Calls per cycle | Median ms | 95th percentile ms | Maximum ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| One | 15 | 19.71 | 26.86 | 49.65 |
| Two | 16 | 18.81 | 31.28 | 43.28 |

**Deviations.** The one-vessel figures were printed to the console. The script failed
later in the same run, before writing its data file, so no file holds them. The
two-vessel figures are in `data/probe_range2_0.json`.

**Conclusions.** A held cycle for one or two vessels fits the game's 20 ms tick at the
median. The tail exceeds it, which in lockstep slows the game rather than losing a step.
Several vessels in flight are still to be measured.
