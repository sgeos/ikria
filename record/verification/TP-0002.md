# TP-0002, Sepratron research program procedure

> **Navigation**: [Verification](README.md)

**Status.** Run on 2026-11-18. Results in [VR-0011](VR-0011.md), program writeup in
[`SEPRATRON_PROGRAM.md`](../../docs/research/SEPRATRON_PROGRAM.md). The operator asked
for the program on 2026-11-18 and named the use cases.

**Save and authority.** "Ikria Physical Verification" only, where the operator allows
debug features. Every script identifies the loaded save with `tools/phase0/guard.py`
and refuses any other. Each debug call is logged in the run's data. The career save was
not loaded.

**Setup.**
1. Checkpoint "ipv-before-sepratron-program", then `Debug.unlock_technology_tree`. The
   call researched every node but recorded no part as purchased, contrary to its
   documentation, so `tools/sepratron/purchase_edit.py` wrote "ipv-sepratron-ready", in
   which every researched node lists its parts as purchased, and the operator loaded it.
2. Test articles are generated by `tools/sepratron/build_articles.py` from stock part
   blocks, reset to each part's defaults. Each revision letter marks a change after a run,
   so a flown article keeps its identity.
3. Before flight, `diag_orientation.py` reads each sepratron's force direction and
   `diag_overlap.py` checks that no sepratron overlaps a part other than its parent.
4. No kerbal flies. A kerbal seated at launch goes on EVA and is recovered before any
   firing, and after an incident a launch first recovers any crewed vessel on the launch
   site.

## Articles

| Article | Configuration | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| IKR-SEP-A1, A1B, A1C | OKTO probe, TR-18A, FL-T100 stage with four retro sepratrons | Separation and deorbit |
| IKR-SEP-A2B, A2C, A2D | Mk1 pod, Mk16 parachute, four upright sepratrons, TR-18A, Flea | Abort |
| IKR-SEP-A3B to A3F | OKTO probe, empty FL-T100 ascent stage with four sepratrons, TR-18A, FL-T100 descent stage with four braking sepratrons, a battery from A3F | Braking and Minmus ascent |
| IKR-SEP-A5, A5B | Mk1 pod, Mk16 parachute, two sepratrons at 0.22 units and a quarter thrust | Soft landing |
| IKR-SEP-B4N, B4R | Stock Aeris 3A with an OKTO probe, without and with four sepratrons | Rocket-assisted takeoff |

## Steps

| Step | Script | Method |
|---|---|---|
| A1, separation and deorbit | `a1_separation.py` | 100 km circular orbit, pointed prograde. Decouple, sample 0.5 s, fire an opposed retro pair, sample through the burn |
| A2, pad abort | `a2_abort.py pad` | Decouple and fire four sepratrons on the pad. Arm the parachute at the apex. A safety net sets the pod down if it is still fast near the ground |
| A2, flight abort | `a2_abort.py flight 0.25 3` | Flea at a quarter thrust, abort after 3 s. One loop guards the pod against the booster, arms the parachute and applies the net |
| A3, Minmus ascent | `a3_minmus_ascent.py` | Flat equatorial site from terrain heights. Decouple to hop, pitch 15 degrees east, fire burn 1, warp to the apoapsis, settle the pointing, circularize |
| B1, capture | `b_braking.py capture` | Hyperbolic approach to Minmus, 15 km periapsis. Fire one pair retrograde, centred on periapsis |
| B2, descent | `b_braking.py descent` | Placed 4 km above the Mun falling at 200 m/s, upright. Fire one pair |
| B3, soft landing | `b_softland.py [height]` | Placed 1,500 m above Kerbin under the parachute. Fire at a radar height |
| B4, takeoff | `b_takeoff.py <article>` | Jet at full throttle for 5 s on the brakes, release, and in B4R fire four sepratrons. Record speed against distance |

Sizing for A3 and A5 is in `tools/sepratron/sizing.py`.

## Deviations

- The A1 and A2 articles first flew with tweaks inherited from stock craft, half-loaded
  sepratrons and a parachute set to open at 50 m, before the generator reset parts to
  their defaults.
- Four articles and one kerbal were lost, reported as
  [I-0003](../incidents/I-0003.md) to [I-0007](../incidents/I-0007.md).
- Two runs left no log, and the first flight abort's log was overwritten by the second
  under the same label. Their figures in VR-0011 come from values printed during the
  session.
