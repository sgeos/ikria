# VR-0003, Extravehicular activity, science and the flag

> **Navigation**: [Verification](./README.md)

**Phase 0 experiments.** 4.

**Purpose.** Establish whether kRPC can send a kerbal outside, take reports and samples,
plant a flag and board again.

**Configuration.** Kerbal Space Program 1.12.5 with both expansions, kRPC 0.7.0 server
and Python client, on an arm64 Mac under macOS 27.2.1. Save "Ikria Simulated Research and
Dev", a sandbox on Hard. Probe scripts are in [`tools/phase0/`](../../tools/phase0/README.md),
in their final versions, and raw outputs in [`data/`](data/). Runs took place on
2026-11-14, in Coordinated Universal Time.

**Procedure.**
1. `probe_pad_eva.py` sent the pod's kerbal outside on the pad with `CrewMember.eva`.
2. `probe_eva_continue.py` listed the kerbal's modules, events and experiments.
3. `probe_eva_actions.py` ran `evaReport` and `surfaceSample` through `Experiment.run`,
   triggered the `PlantFlag` event, boarded with `Control.board`, and read the pod's
   science container.
4. `probe_range2.py` released the ladder with `Control.release_ladder`, walked the
   kerbal 21.7 m clear, and triggered `PlantFlag` again. A save made through kRPC was
   then read for vessels.

**Results.**
- The extravehicular report and the surface sample both took data. Their subjects were
  "from LaunchPad", one of the Space Center's location biomes, with caps of 1.44 and
  7.2. Those equal the base caps of 8 and 40, times Kerbin's landed multiplier of 0.3,
  times 0.6, which confirms that Hard's science multiplier scales each subject.
- Boarding stored both results in the pod's science container.
- kRPC has no call to plant a flag. The kerbal module's `PlantFlag` event, inactive on
  the ladder, became active with the kerbal standing clear and carrying a flag.
  Triggering it planted a flag, which the save file listed as a landed vessel.

**Deviations.**
- The first runs used an earlier draft of `probe_pad_eva.py`, which called a module
  attribute that kRPC 0.7.0 does not have. The draft was fixed.
- One flag trial triggered the event while it was inactive, on the pad, and planted
  nothing.
- One kerbal carried no flag, so a second trial proved nothing.
- A walk failed while the kerbal held the ladder.
- Reading `available` on the Breaking Ground experiment `ROCScience` raised a null
  reference inside kRPC, which is a defect in kRPC.

**Conclusions.** Reports, samples, boarding and flags all work through kRPC. A flag
needs the kerbal clear of the ladder and carrying a flag.
