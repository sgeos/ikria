# VR-0007, The kRPC client interface

> **Navigation**: [Verification](./README.md)

**Phase 0 experiments.** 5 and 6.

**Purpose.** Establish from the kRPC 0.7.0 Python client's interface what the harness can
read and do, without changing any save.

**Configuration.** The kRPC 0.7.0 Python client, its generated `spacecenter.py` and
`debug.py`.

**Procedure.** Read the client's procedures and their descriptions.

**Results.**
- No facility levels are exposed, so the harness reads them from the save file.
- The agency's funds, science and reputation, and each contract's advance, completion
  and failure rewards, are exposed. No record or event of why funds change is.
- `launch_vessel` takes a craft directory, name and site, then a crew list, then the
  recover flag.
- The scene is `game_scene`, not `current_game_scene`.
- The `KRPC.Debug` service sets funds, science and reputation, upgrades facilities,
  unlocks the tree, and moves vessels into orbits or onto the ground.
- There is no KerbNet or anomaly interface.

**Deviations.** None.

**Conclusions.**
- The harness enforces facility gates itself under R2.
- The financial ledger infers each transaction from snapshots around events.
- The harness bars the debug service from the campaign and allows it only in the
  verification save.
- Finding a Green Monolith needs KerbNet's in-game window.
